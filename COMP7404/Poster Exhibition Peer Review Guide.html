Poster Exhibition Peer Review Guide

Why We Are Doing This

During the exhibition, each student is assigned to submit two peer reviews. These are formative reviews. The purpose is not to grade your peers. The purpose is to help them see how their poster and explanation come across to another student.

This matters because a poster is not just a piece of coursework. It is a communication exercise. A strong poster should help someone quickly understand:

  • what problem the project addresses
  • why it matters
  • what the main idea is
  • what the audience should remember afterwards

Your role as a reviewer is to give feedback that is specific, fair, and useful.

What You Should Do

Please review the two specific peers assigned to you for your exhibition session.

For each assigned presenter:

  1. Visit the poster.
  2. Listen carefully to the explanation.
  3. Ask at least one genuine question.
  4. Complete the Moodle peer review form individually.

The review should reflect your own judgment. Do not submit a group review on behalf of your teammates.

What Good Feedback Looks Like

Good feedback is:

  • specific rather than vague
  • constructive rather than harsh
  • focused on communication and clarity
  • honest but respectful

Examples of useful feedback:

  • “The motivation was clear, but I could not tell what was new compared with prior work.”
  • “The main figure helped a lot. I understood the idea much faster once the presenter pointed to it.”
  • “The explanation of the evaluation results was too quick. I was not sure what the numbers actually showed.”

Examples of weak feedback:

  • “Good job.”
  • “Nice poster.”
  • “Too confusing.”

Those comments are too short to help anyone improve.

What You Should Pay Attention To

As you review a poster, think about questions like these:

  • Could I understand the project goal quickly?
  • Was the main contribution clear?
  • Was the presenter able to explain their own role clearly?
  • Did the poster help communication, or did it make things harder?
  • What was strong?
  • What was missing or confusing?

Important Boundary

This is a formative review. The final summative judgment for grading will be done by the TA.

That means:

  • you are not deciding the project grade
  • you are helping your peers improve
  • your comments should aim to support learning, not to punish mistakes
